# Datasets

* [Generated artefacts](generated-artefacts.md) - What `just gen-project` and `just gen-doc` generate from the schema: the projections in `project/`, the Python models in `src/nist_ai_100_1/datamodel/`, and the merged schema in `docs/schema/`. None is edited by hand.
* [Neighbours' adapt copies](neighbour-copies.md) - The adapt copies of 19 released lmodel repositories in `tests/neighbours/`, among them oscal, nist-csf-v2 and iso27001, each pinned by its SHA-256, against which `tests/test_schema_rules.py` holds this schema's copy apart and checks the mappings.
* [Example data](example-data.md) - The examples `tests/test_data.py` checks: a valid example of each class that is not abstract, and a counter-example for each constraint, whose first line names where it must fail.
* [nist-ai-100-1 LinkML schema](nist-ai-100-1-linkml-schema.md) - The LinkML model of NIST AI 100-1, `src/nist_ai_100_1/schema/nist_ai_100_1.yaml`: an umbrella over four modules that holds the tree root `AiRmfFramework`, with the `AiRmf` class prefix and the `ai_rmf_` slot prefix.
* [Base module](base-module.md) - `nist_ai_100_1_base.yaml`, the slice a profile can import alone: the identifier forms, the enums of trustworthiness characteristics and AI actor tasks, the `title` and `description` slots, and the abstract `AiRmfRecord`.
* [Foundations module](foundations-module.md) - `nist_ai_100_1_foundations.yaml`: classes and enums for Part 1 and Appendices A to D, such as lifecycle stages, measurement challenges and AI-specific risks, and the classes under `AiRmfRecord` for an adopter's systems, actors, risks, impacts, harms, tolerances and biases.
* [AI RMF Core module](rmf-core-module.md) - `nist_ai_100_1_rmf_core.yaml`: `AiRmfFunction`, `AiRmfCategory` and `AiRmfSubcategory`, with the Playbook material a subcategory holds, in `AiRmfSuggestedAction`, `AiRmfDocumentationItem` and `AiRmfResource`.
* [Profiles module](profiles-module.md) - `nist_ai_100_1_profiles.yaml`: `AiRmfProfile` and `AiRmfProfileKind`, for the use-case, current, target and cross-sectoral profiles of section 6. The publication prescribes no template, so only the kinds and the subcategories are structured.
* [The catalogue](catalogue.md) - `src/nist_ai_100_1/data/ai-rmf-1.0.yaml`, the whole framework as one `AiRmfFramework` record: 4 functions, 19 categories, 72 subcategories, 575 suggested actions and 292 documentation prompts, and the lists of Part 1 and the appendices.
* [Copy of the upstream](upstream-copy.md) - `upstream/`: the publication's PDF, NIST's OSCAL catalog with its licence, and the Playbook's file, unchanged. `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` pins each by hash, and `tests/test_upstream.py` compares them offline.
* [Where NIST's catalog departs from the publication](catalog-departures.md) - `tests/catalog_departures.yaml`: the 44 of 91 statements that NIST's OSCAL catalog words differently from Tables 1 to 4 of the publication, each with both wordings. `tests/test_catalogue.py` fails on a new difference.
* [The schema's adapt copy](adapt-copy.md) - `.lokf/nist-ai-100-1.yaml`, the copy `just lokf-adapt` writes from the schema so that it sits beside LOKF's `lokf.yaml`: 13 classes rooted on `Concept`, `ai_rmf_id` dropped for LOKF's `id`, and `title` and `description` folded into LOKF's.
* [GOVERN 1.1](example-subcategory.md) - Subcategory GOVERN 1.1 of the AI RMF, written as a concept of type `AiRmfSubcategory` in the adapt copy's keys. It shows that the domain schema admits a subcategory as a concept.
