# Modelling choices

The publication sets no scale for likelihood or magnitude, and the Playbook allows qualitative ones, so both are text.[^about]

Appendix A separates AI actor tasks from additional AI actors, but the Playbook's AI Actors key and AI 600-1's AI Actor Tasks tag both together, so one enum holds both. Each record says which it is.[^about]

Figure 2 shows six lifecycle stages, and Figure 3 adds Use or Impacted by under People and Planet.[^about]

Section 6 does not say profile kinds exclude each other, so a profile's kinds are a list.[^about]
