# NIST AI 100-1, the AI RMF 1.0

Part 1 frames risk, its audience and the trustworthiness characteristics. Part 2 sets out the Core and profiles. Appendices A to D describe AI actor tasks, how AI risks differ from traditional software risks, human-AI interaction and the attributes of the framework.[^publication]

NIST numbers the framework 1.0 and plans a review no later than 2028.[^publication]
