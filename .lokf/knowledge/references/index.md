# References

* [NIST AI 100-1, the AI RMF 1.0](nist-ai-100-1.md) - The publication this schema renders: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), NIST AI 100-1, January 2023, with Part 1, the Core and profiles in Part 2, and Appendices A to D. `upstream/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf` holds it.
* [NIST's catalog of the AI RMF in OSCAL](oscal-catalog.md) - NIST's Open Security Controls Assessment Language (OSCAL) catalog of the AI RMF 1.0 in usnistgov/oscal-content at v1.6.0: the Core with each function's overview and the Playbook's material, restated from the publication with some differences.
* [The NIST AI RMF Playbook](ai-rmf-playbook.md) - The online companion to the AI RMF, with suggested actions and resources for each subcategory. `upstream/playbook.json` holds its file, which adds the AI actor and topic tags that NIST's catalog lacks. NIST updates it apart from the publication.
