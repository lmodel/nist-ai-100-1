# Glossary

* [Adapt copy](adapt-copy.md) - An adapt copy: the copy of a published LinkML schema that `lokf adapt` writes so that it can sit beside LOKF's `lokf.yaml` in a domain schema, with its chosen roots on `Concept`.
* [AI RMF](ai-rmf.md) - The AI Risk Management Framework: NIST's voluntary framework for managing the risks of AI systems, published as NIST AI 100-1. Its Core has four functions.
* [Function](function.md) - A function of the AI RMF Core: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE or MANAGE, which organize AI risk management at the highest level. GOVERN cuts across the other three.
* [Subcategory](subcategory.md) - A specific outcome in the AI RMF Core, such as GOVERN 1.1, named by its category's identifier and a number. There are 72.
* [Trustworthiness characteristic](trustworthiness-characteristic.md) - One of seven characteristics of trustworthy AI systems in section 3, from valid and reliable to fair with harmful bias managed. Figure 4 makes valid and reliable the base.
* [AI actor](ai-actor.md) - An organization or individual with an active role in an AI system's lifecycle. Appendix A describes ten AI actor tasks and five additional AI actors.
* [Playbook](playbook.md) - The NIST AI RMF Playbook: an online companion that suggests actions and resources for each subcategory, voluntary and updated apart from the publication.
* [AI RMF profile](profile.md) - An implementation of the Core for a setting, application or technology, or a description of a current or target state, or a treatment of risks across sectors. Section 6 prescribes no template.
* [Residual risk](residual-risk.md) - The risk that remains after treatment, in section 1.2.3. MANAGE 1.4 calls negative residual risks the sum of all unmitigated risks, so the publication has two definitions.
